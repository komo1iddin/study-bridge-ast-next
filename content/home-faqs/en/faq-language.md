question: "Do I need to know Chinese to study in China?"
answer: "It depends on your chosen program. For Chinese-taught programs, an HSK certificate (usually level 4-5) is required. For English-taught programs, Chinese knowledge is not mandatory, but an IELTS or TOEFL certificate is required. Many universities offer one-year preparatory language courses before starting the main degree program."
