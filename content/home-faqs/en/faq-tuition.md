question: "How much does tuition cost at Chinese universities?"
answer: "Tuition fees range from $2,000 to $10,000 per year depending on the university, program, and language of instruction. English-taught programs are usually more expensive. Prestigious universities in Beijing and Shanghai have higher tuition fees. Medicine and engineering programs tend to be the most expensive, while humanities and Chinese language programs are more affordable."
