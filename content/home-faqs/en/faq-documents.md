question: "What documents do I need for a Chinese student visa?"
answer: "For a student visa, you'll need: admission letter from the university, JW201/JW202 form, valid passport (with at least 6 months validity), completed visa application form, passport-sized photos, medical examination report, and proof of financial capability. All documents must be translated into Chinese or English and notarized."
