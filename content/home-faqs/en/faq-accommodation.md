question: "How is student accommodation organized in China?"
answer: "Most international students live in university dormitories, where single or double rooms are available. Costs vary from $60 to $300 per month depending on the city and conditions. Dormitories are usually equipped with internet, air conditioning, shared kitchen, and laundry facilities. Alternatively, renting an apartment off-campus is typically more expensive but provides more independence."
