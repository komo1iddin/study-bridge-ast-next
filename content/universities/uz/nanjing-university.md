# Nanjing University

Nanjing University (NJU), founded in 1902, is one of China's oldest and most prestigious educational institutions. As a member of the elite C9 League, NJU is renowned for its balanced strengths across humanities, social sciences, natural sciences, medicine, and engineering, with particularly distinguished programs in literature, history, physics, and chemistry.

## Programs for International Students

Nanjing University offers a diverse range of programs for international students:

- **Undergraduate Programs**: Over 15 bachelor's degree programs available in Chinese and English
- **Graduate Programs**: Comprehensive master's and doctoral programs across 75+ disciplines
- **Joint Programs**: 2+2 arrangements with partner universities in Europe, America, and Asia
- **Research Opportunities**: Collaborative research projects in NJU's numerous research institutes
- **Chinese Language Programs**: Ranging from short-term intensive courses to year-long programs
- **Summer Schools**: Interdisciplinary programs combining academic study with cultural experiences

## Rich Historical Legacy

Nanjing University's history reflects China's educational evolution:

- Originated from Sanjiang Normal School founded in 1902
- Evolved through various incarnations including National Central University
- Became Nanjing University in 1950
- Merged with other prestigious institutions including Jinling University
- Preserved cultural and educational traditions through various historical periods

This rich heritage gives NJU a unique academic atmosphere that blends traditional Chinese scholarly values with modern educational approaches.

## Campus Life

NJU operates three beautiful campuses in Nanjing:

- **Gulou Campus**: The historic main campus in downtown Nanjing, featuring traditional Chinese architecture blended with modern facilities
- **Xianlin Campus**: The modern, expansive main campus with state-of-the-art facilities
- **Pukou Campus**: Focus on specific research areas and continuing education

The university provides well-equipped libraries housing over 4.5 million volumes, modern research laboratories, comfortable student accommodation, and excellent sports facilities. NJU supports over 100 student organizations covering academic interests, arts, sports, and community service.

## Academic Strengths and Research

Nanjing University is particularly renowned for:

- Humanities and social sciences, particularly Chinese literature, history, and philosophy
- Natural sciences, with physics, chemistry, and astronomy among the top programs in China
- Earth sciences and atmospheric research
- Computer science and artificial intelligence

The university hosts numerous national key laboratories, including:
- State Key Laboratory of Pharmaceutical Biotechnology
- State Key Laboratory of Coordination Chemistry
- State Key Laboratory for Novel Software Technology

## International Collaboration

Nanjing University has established partnerships with over 250 universities and research institutions across 40+ countries. The university hosts several joint institutes including:

- NJU-Johns Hopkins University Center for Chinese and American Studies
- Nanjing-Nottingham Center
- Sino-German Institute of Social Sciences

NJU's alumni include numerous Nobel Prize winners, renowned scholars, business leaders, and government officials who have made significant contributions to China and the international community.
