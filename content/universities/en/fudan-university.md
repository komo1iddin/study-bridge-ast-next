# Fudan University

Fudan University, established in 1905, is one of China's most prestigious and selective universities. Located in Shanghai, it has consistently ranked among the top universities in Asia and maintains a strong international reputation, particularly in medicine, business, and international relations.

## Programs for International Students

Fudan offers comprehensive programs for international students:

- **Bachelor's Programs**: Available in Chinese and English, spanning humanities, sciences, medicine, and business
- **Master's Programs**: Over 40 disciplines taught in English
- **Doctoral Programs**: Research-focused programs across multiple disciplines
- **Exchange Programs**: 1+2 and semester-based exchange with over 200 partner universities globally
- **Chinese Language Programs**: Short and long-term language immersion programs

## Campus Life

Fudan's main campus is situated in the Yangpu District of Shanghai, with four campuses throughout the city:

- **Handan Campus**: Main campus featuring classic Chinese architecture and modern facilities
- **Jiangwan Campus**: Newest campus with state-of-the-art research facilities
- **Zhangjiang Campus**: Science and technology focused campus
- **Fenglin Campus**: Home to the renowned Fudan University Shanghai Medical College

The university has over 30 residence halls, extensive sports facilities, and over 200 student organizations, providing a dynamic environment for both academic and personal development.

## International Recognition

Fudan University has partnerships with over 200 universities worldwide and hosts visiting scholars and exchange students from across the globe. It is a member of the prestigious C9 League (China's Ivy League) and Universitas 21, a global network of research universities.

The university's Shanghai Medical College is particularly renowned, with affiliated hospitals that rank among the best in China. The Fudan School of Management holds AACSB, EQUIS, and AMBA accreditation, placing it among the elite business schools worldwide.
