# Zhejiang University

Zhejiang University (ZJU), founded in 1897, is one of China's oldest and most prestigious higher education institutions. Located in Hangzhou, a city known for its natural beauty and as a technology hub, ZJU is a member of the elite C9 League and has consistently ranked among the top universities in Asia.

## Programs for International Students

ZJU offers a wide range of programs for international students:

- **Undergraduate Programs**: Over 15 English-taught bachelor's degree programs across engineering, science, agriculture, medicine, humanities, and social sciences
- **Graduate Programs**: 40+ English master's programs and doctoral research opportunities 
- **Exchange Programs**: Extensive 1+2 and 2+2 programs with over 190 partner institutions worldwide
- **Non-Degree Programs**: Visiting student options and Chinese language courses
- **Summer Schools**: Various interdisciplinary programs focused on cutting-edge research areas

## Campus Life

Zhejiang University spans 7 campuses across Hangzhou:

- **Yuquan Campus**: The historic main campus with classical architecture
- **Xixi Campus**: Houses arts and humanities departments
- **Huajiachi Campus**: Focus on agricultural sciences and life sciences
- **Zijingang Campus**: The modern center of the university with comprehensive facilities
- **Zhijiang Campus**: Base for international education
- **Haining International Campus**: Newest campus for international cooperation and joint institutes
- **Zhoushan Campus**: Focus on ocean sciences

The university offers excellent accommodation, libraries with over 7 million volumes, advanced laboratories, and extensive recreational facilities. ZJU has a vibrant student community with over 300 student organizations spanning academic interests, arts, sports, and public service.

## Research Excellence

ZJU has established itself as a research powerhouse with:

- 15 state key laboratories and engineering research centers
- 14 national engineering technology centers
- 3 national engineering laboratories

The university is particularly strong in:
- Computer science and artificial intelligence
- Advanced manufacturing
- Clean energy
- Biotechnology and medicine
- Agricultural sciences

## International Collaboration

Zhejiang University has developed partnerships with over 190 institutions across more than 80 countries. The university hosts several joint institutes, including:

- China-UK Institute of Clean Technology
- ZJU-UIUC Institute (with University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign)
- ZJU-Edinburgh Institute
- Zhejiang University/University of Edinburgh Institute of Biomedical Sciences

The university has nurtured numerous distinguished alumni who have made significant contributions in academia, business, and public service both in China and internationally.
