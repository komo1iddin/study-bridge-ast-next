# Shanghai Jiao Tong University

Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), founded in 1896, is one of China's oldest and most prestigious higher education institutions. With a strong emphasis on engineering, science, medicine, and management, SJTU has established itself as a world-class comprehensive research university.

## Programs for International Students

SJTU offers diverse academic opportunities for international students:

- **Undergraduate Programs**: Over 20 English-taught bachelor's programs in engineering, science, medicine, and business
- **Graduate Programs**: 35+ English master's programs and doctoral research opportunities
- **Joint Degree Programs**: 2+2 programs with top universities worldwide
- **Summer Schools**: Intensive programs combining academic study with cultural experiences
- **Chinese Language Programs**: Courses for all proficiency levels

## Campus Life

SJTU is spread across five campuses in Shanghai:

- **Xuhui Campus**: The original campus with historic architecture and central location
- **Minhang Campus**: The modern main campus with comprehensive facilities
- **Qibao Campus**: Home to the School of Medicine
- **Luwan Campus**: Base for continuing education
- **Lin Gang Campus**: The newest campus focused on science and innovation

The university provides excellent student facilities including modern dormitories, libraries with over 4.8 million volumes, state-of-the-art laboratories, and extensive sports facilities. SJTU hosts more than 250 student organizations and clubs, fostering a vibrant campus culture.

## Academic Strength and Research

SJTU is particularly renowned for:

- Engineering and technology programs consistently ranked among China's top three
- The SJTU School of Medicine (formerly Shanghai Medical University)
- Antai College of Economics & Management, triple-accredited by AACSB, EQUIS, and AMBA
- Shanghai Advanced Institute of Finance

The university operates over 200 research centers and institutes, including national laboratories and engineering research centers. SJTU contributes significantly to China's advancement in areas like naval architecture, microelectronics, and artificial intelligence.

## International Recognition

SJTU is a founding member of the prestigious C9 League (China's equivalent to the Ivy League) and has established partnerships with more than 280 universities and institutions worldwide. The university has nurtured numerous distinguished alumni, including notable scientists, business leaders, and political figures who have made significant contributions globally.
